# Example Co.

A **fictional** brand that ships with the repo so every template renders out of the box. It is not
a real company. It shows what a filled-in brand looks like; keep it as a reference or delete it.

## Logo

- Light backgrounds: `logo.svg`. Forest green: `logo-reversed.svg`. Ember and photographs:
  `logo-white.svg`.
- Keep the sun and the name together. Never recolor, stretch, rotate or outline either.

## Color

- Cream and espresso carry most pieces. Ember is the accent: highlights, buttons, numbers.
- Forest green is the dark theme, for stories and closing slides.
- Ember text on cream is for headlines and labels; set long text in espresso.

## Type

- Fraunces for headlines and quotes, in sentence case. Highlighted words go in Fraunces italic.
- Inter for everything else.

## Voice

- Warm and plain. Short sentences. Say what happens, when and where.
- Prefer "come see" and "join us" to "don't miss" and "exclusive".

## Don'ts

- No exclamation marks in headlines.
- No more than one highlighted phrase per headline.
