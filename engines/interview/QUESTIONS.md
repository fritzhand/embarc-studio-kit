# The seeded interview

A podcast-style conversation in which a host asks you a set list of questions, so the recording holds every piece
of your company's story. One 20 to 30 minute session gives you a founder video, a set of short clips, quotes for
posts, and the words for your website.

Bring someone to ask the questions: a co-founder, a teammate, or another founder. Answering a person is easier,
and sounds more natural, than talking to a lens.

## Before you start

- Send the host this page a day ahead. Neither of you needs a script; the host needs the questions.
- Answer in full sentences that carry the question in them. "We started Example Co. because…" works on its own
  as a clip. "Because…" needs the question to make sense.
- Keep each answer to 30 to 90 seconds. If an answer runs long, the host asks for "the short version" and you
  answer again.
- The host stays silent while you answer: no "mm-hm" or "right". Silence lets the editor cut cleanly.
- Look at the host, not the camera.
- Say the one-sentence version of what you build twice, in two takes. It becomes your opening line.
- Use real figures only, and say where each one comes from. If you are not sure of a number, leave it out.

## The questions

### 1. Who you are

1. Tell us your name, your company, and your role there.
2. What did you do before this?
3. What in your background prepared you to build this company?

### 2. What you are building

4. Describe what you build in one sentence, the way you would say it to a friend. *(Two takes.)*
5. Walk us through how someone uses it, from the first minute.
6. What does it do that people could not do before?

### 3. Why

7. Why this problem? When did you first notice it?
8. Why now? What changed that makes this the right time?
9. Why you? What do you know about this that others do not?

### 4. Who it is for

10. Who is your customer? Describe one real person, by their job and their day, not their name.
11. What were they doing before you came along?
12. What does it cost them, in time, money, or stress, when the problem goes unsolved?

### 5. Why they should care

13. What changes for that person once they use your product?
14. What is a moment from a customer that stayed with you? *(Only one you have permission to tell.)*
15. If that person watches this video, what should they do next?

### 6. Closing

16. Where is the company going in the next year?
17. Anything we did not ask that you want to say?
18. Say your company's name and website, slowly, once. *(Used for the end card and checking the spelling.)*

## After the session

1. Take the recording home (the studio's quick start shows how).
2. Put it in `projects/<your company>/footage/` in this kit and run
   `npm run transcribe -- projects/<your company>/footage/<file>.mp4 projects/<your company>/transcript.json --list`.
3. Ask your agent to pick soundbites for each section above (see `AGENTS.md`, "Founder video").
